Organize edits and create chapters or rhythm-based cuts.

## Using this feature {#usage}

M adds a marker at the playhead. Markers hold name, note, color and optional region duration. Drag on the ruler to move one; double-click renames it. The Markers panel offers thumbnails, navigation, CSV and rendered images. A region can set the work area or be exported individually.
