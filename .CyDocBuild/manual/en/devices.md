Use CyDAW-style insert chains with editable and automatable controls.

## Using this feature {#usage}

```steps
{"items": [{"title": "Step 1", "body": "Select an audio clip or press its track FX button to show Track Effects for that channel. Choose track, bus or master deliberately."}, {"title": "Step 2", "body": "Use + Add device to search a family or name. Power bypasses a device; drag to reorder and remove to delete. The internal catalogue offers 50 stereo insert effects: dynamics, EQ/filters, reverbs and convolution, delays, saturation, amp/cabinet/microphone simulation, channel strip/mastering, pitch processing, modulation, resonators, utility and meters. Instruments, MIDI effects, modulators and CyDAW containers are not offered as insert devices here.
```
