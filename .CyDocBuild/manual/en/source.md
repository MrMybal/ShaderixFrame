Mark the useful part of a source before placing it in a sequence.

## Using this feature {#usage}

```steps
{"items": [{"title": "Step 1", "body": "Double-click a bin card to open Source. Scrub its range bar or play with Space. I marks In and O marks Out; drag their handles for adjustment."}, {"title": "Step 2", "body": "Choose picture, audio or both. Use a track header’s C button to target the destination tracks. Place the sequence playhead where the edit should begin."}, {"title": "Step 3", "body": "Insert (comma) places the marked range and shifts later material. Overwrite (period) replaces the destination interval. Drag the source image onto a timeline track for direct placement."}, {"title": "Step 4", "body": "Fit to Fill (Shift+period) changes speed to fill the sequence work area. Check motion and sound after a speed change. Replace Clip keeps the clip’s placement and duration while replacing its source."}, {"title": "Step 5", "body": "Select an existing timeline clip and press F for Match Frame: Source opens at that source range, ready for another edit."}]}
```

```callout
{"type": "note", "title": "Note", "body": "Keyboard meaning follows focus. In Source, I/O mark source points and K stops source playback; on the main timeline I/O define the work area and L toggles looping. Click the intended panel before using shortcuts."}
```
