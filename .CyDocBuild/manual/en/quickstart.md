A complete path from an example or imported footage to a saved project and a rendered movie.

## Using this feature {#usage}

```steps
{"items": [{"title": "Step 1", "body": "Open File > Open Project and choose examples/CutStudy.sframe to learn without preparing media. Alternatively create a sequence from File > New from Template, then import footage with Ctrl+I or drag files into the window."}, {"title": "Step 2", "body": "Press Space to play or stop. Click the timeline ruler to seek. Select a clip, drag its body to move it and drag its edges to trim. Press S to split a selected clip at the playhead; Ctrl+Z undoes a change."}, {"title": "Step 3", "body": "Open Properties to adjust position, scale, opacity or clip effects. Keep linked video and audio together for ordinary edits; the selection includes their linked members."}, {"title": "Step 4", "body": "Add a title from the timeline add menu, edit its text in Properties, then move and trim the title clip. Set the playhead to two different times and use K beside a parameter to create animation."}, {"title": "Step 5", "body": "Save with Ctrl+S as a .sframe project. Open Exports, select MP4 H264/AAC, choose a destination and start the job. Follow its progress and play the finished file to check picture and sound."}]}
```
