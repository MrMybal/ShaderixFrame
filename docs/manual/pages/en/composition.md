Inspect the final image without changing the scale of the clips.

## Using this feature {#usage}

Wheel zooms around the pointer. Fit shows the whole image, 1:1 shows source pixels and the menu offers 25–1600%. Ctrl+0 fits, Ctrl+1 sets 100%, Ctrl with plus/minus zooms. H over the image selects Hand; middle-drag, Space-drag, Shift+wheel, horizontal touchpad scroll and the margin drag pan the view. The minimap indicates and moves the visible area.

Preview rendering follows the viewing zoom up to sequence resolution. Above 200%, pixel display becomes sharp for inspection. Viewer zoom is a navigation setting: use the clip’s Scale property to resize actual content. Guides can show safe action/title areas, thirds, center and a target aspect ratio.
